Part I

The running order

Four pubs, none more than five minutes apart. Sunset on the canal, an old saloon bar, DJs, then a cosy finish until one.

Pizza at ours at 11pm. Until then, keep going on sides: open "Snacks here" on a stop for prices.

Part II

Good to know

A few small things that make the night easier for everyone.

  1. I
    Water at every stop. Tap water is free in any UK pub. Just ask.
  2. II
    Leave whenever you like. Just tell someone, so we're not waiting for you at the headcount before the next pub.
  3. III
    Bring physical ID. Challenge 25 is a thing. Passport for all non-UK residents. Photos on your phone won't count.
Part III

Getting home

Transport and safety for the end of the night.

Night Tube
Northern line from Camden Town, all night on Saturdays. Charing Cross branch only, not Bank. About every 8 minutes.
Night buses
Night (N) and 24-hour routes run from Camden High St and Camden Town station. Check Citymapper or TfL Go for your route.
Overground
No overnight service from Camden Road. Check the last train time before you head out.
Clocks go back
At 2am on Sunday 25 October, clocks go back to 1am. Phones update automatically.
Phones
Camden has one of the highest phone theft rates in the UK.
